## 0:00 to 1:00: The challenge and what we built

- Most drug and alcohol help closes at 5pm
- People are most alone at night
- Over 80% of Scottish drug services are closed at weekends (one charity report)
- We built a digital first responder: no name, nothing saved
- It shows what's open now, what opens soon, and 24-hour lines
- Nobody gets sent to a closed door

## 1:00 to 2:30: Demo: the mum at 3am

Set **Pretend the time is** to **Sunday 3am** first.

1. **No, not right now → Someone I care about.** Her son, 20, hasn't come home.
2. **I don't know what to do or say.** Tonight's steps, tomorrow's talk, no blame.
3. **Find help for them → Help for them to cut down or stop.** No location asked: nothing opens soon.
4. **Results:** nothing open, 24-hour lines, family support at 10am.

Close: *"At 3am, help really does run out."*
