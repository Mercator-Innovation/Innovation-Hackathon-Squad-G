# Show and tell: Squad Gusto (2 minutes)

**Challenge 2: Help when it's needed.** Presenters: _(to confirm)_

## 0:00 to 1:00: What we built, and for whom

- **Get the support you need** is a digital first responder for anyone affected by substance use, day or night. It asks a few questions with no name and nothing saved, then shows **what's open now, then what opens in the next two hours**, plus 24-hour lines. Nobody gets sent to a closed door.
- **Scenario A, Sunday 3am:** a mum's 20-year-old hasn't come home again after Saturday night. She picks "Someone I care about", then "I don't know what to do or say". She gets what to do tonight and how to talk tomorrow, without blame. Then "Find help for them": it's honest that nothing near her is open, shows the 24-hour lines, and tells her family support opens at 10am.
- **Scenario B, Friday 7:30pm:** someone is feeling twitchy and wants to stay sober tonight. They pick "Me", "Someone to talk to", "My drinking". Drinkline and a web chat are open now, and peer support **opens at 8pm, in 30 minutes**.

## 1:00 to 1:30: One thing that didn't work

- **We nearly showed the wrong help.** Testing scenario B as the person, the first results were drop-ins offering clean injecting equipment: the wrong thing for someone staying away from temptation. Eloise fixed it minutes before the hard stop, but there's still no "help me stay sober tonight" option.
- **And at 3am, help really does run out.** We built a "Where help runs out" page so services can see the gaps.

## 1:30 to 2:00: One surprise from the tooling

- **Chay, our QA engineer, built a voice companion** that talks and listens. That's normally a developer's job.
- **Five of us made 35 changes in about two hours**, including Gaelic and Scots, a low battery mode, and checked sources on every real helpline.
